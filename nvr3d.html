HOW TO PLAY

  1. There are five rooms. Each one hides 6 items.
  2. ✦ Sparkles mark a clock, a painting, a mirror, a sheet of paper or a paint pot with an item inside. Press E (on a tablet, tap USE) and answer the question.
  3. You get two tries. Right first time scores a point. Press H or the How? button for the method.
  4. With 6 items, the door to the next room opens. In the last room, beat the Smudge in a duel, then step through the golden frame.
