Station Zero

DEEP SPACE · DAY 412

HOW TO PLAY

  1. There are five modules. Each one hides 6 items.
  2. ✦ Sparkles mark a locker, a pod, a console or a crate with an item inside. Press E (on a tablet, tap USE) and answer the question.
  3. You get two tries. Right first time scores a point. Press H or the How? button for the method.
  4. With 6 items, the door to the next module opens. On the bridge, beat COGITO in a logic duel, then reach the escape pod.
